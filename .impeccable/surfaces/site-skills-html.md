## Evidence contract

Keep the three axes distinct. The token result is 8,026 net text tokens saved
under `o200k_base` across three qualifying development excerpts, after the
counted skill, discovery, and invocation overhead. The correctness evidence
covers ten scoped preservation checks on 24 archived-text replays plus 25
runtime tests. The performance result is measured wrapper overhead across
140 synthetic native/wrapper pairs: 41.82 ms median and 48.37 ms p95 on the
reported shared host. These results do not establish billed savings, better
diagnosis, higher whole-task success, or faster completion.

Retain the negative evidence alongside the savings. All 14 selected replays
from the previously unused cohort were below the 8 KiB routing threshold;
unnecessary use would add an estimated 3,612 first-use text tokens. The CI
pilot's two repeated groups did not establish avoidable model polling, so
`system-one-ci` remains research. Always-visible discovery overhead on unused
tasks remains unmeasured.

Admission requires comparison with the best practical native tool on unseen
complete tasks, independent correctness checks, all relevant overhead, and
unused tasks. The linked trial protocol requires at least 30 independently
audited task/session clusters per declared scope. Correctness failures or
material latency regressions block adoption regardless of token savings.
Keep the primary evidence links pinned to source revision `bb0d91d`.
Distinguish the September 19, 2026 source-check date from runtime v0.4.0.
