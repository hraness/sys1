What is included, and how to read it

The chart includes the built-in Jev provider and the documented OpenJev compatible-server route. All three bundled Qwen models remain visible as unscored. djev, SemIf, Verdict, and Laya belong in the broader research comparison; sharing weights or answer types does not make them a bundled Sys1 integration.

Accuracy uses 220 hard items. Intelligence weights results across 534 decisions. Calibration evaluates hard-tier distributions. The overall score is the geometric mean of intelligence, calibration, speed, and cost, weighted equally; it is not an accuracy percentage.

This is an English pilot with language-model-authored and reviewed hard cases, mixed serving environments, and one-run estimates without headline uncertainty intervals. Held-out items are sent to evaluated providers. The overall score uses estimated costs and a ×2 + 0.15 second adjustment for self-hosted latency. Evaluate the exact route on your own decisions before enabling it.

About the accuracy chart on openjev.com

openjev.com is SemIf, formerly called OpenJev. Its authored, perturbed, and TypeSafe-subset results use different methods and adapters. It is separate from the razorback16 DiffusionGemma server. Sys1 can run some of the same Qwen model families, but those figures do not measure Sys1’s quantized GGUF path, so we do not transfer them into this chart.
